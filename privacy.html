Privacy Policy

Art of Power: Daily Wisdom · Effective date: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

The Art of Power ("the app") is made by chmays.app, an independent developer based in Denmark. If you have questions about privacy, contact us at support@chmays.app.

2. The short version

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. The app has no account, no login, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

3. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device: your progress (completed lessons, scenarios, challenges and program days), your points and streak, the notes you write, and your settings, such as the daily reminder time. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Depending on your iPhone settings, it may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup), which are controlled by Apple and by you.

4. The AI Advisor

On supported iPhones, the AI Advisor uses Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). What you type is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us. We do not see, store or receive your questions or the answers. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, the Advisor uses guided answers that are built into the app, and nothing is sent anywhere.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your iPhone. No push server is used, and no data is sent to us. You can turn the reminder off at any time in the app's Settings or in iPhone Settings.

6. Sharing and contact

When you use Share or Export, the iPhone share sheet opens and you decide where the text goes. If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send, and we use it only to answer you.

7. Third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Apple processes App Store downloads and crash reports you choose to share with developers, according to Apple's own privacy policy.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

9. Deleting your data

You can delete all your progress and notes at any time in Settings → Reset all data. Deleting the app also removes all its data from your iPhone.

10. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EU or EEA, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. In Denmark, this is Datatilsynet.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it in the app and change the effective date above.

12. Contact
